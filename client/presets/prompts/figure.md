Look at the attached figure.

1. **What it shows:** type of figure, panels, modality (CT, histology, plot, diagram).
2. **Read it:** axes, units, scale bars, legends, sample sizes, error bars and what they mean.
3. **Main message:** what a reader should take from it, in one or two sentences.
4. **Check it:** missing units or scale bars, unreadable labels, misleading axes or colours,
   error bars not defined, panels not referenced, a claim the figure doesn't support.
5. **Fixes:** a concrete fix for each problem.

Say "unreadable" rather than guessing a value.
